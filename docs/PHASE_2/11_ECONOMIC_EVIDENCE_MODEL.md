# METATRON WORKFORCE — PHASE 2 / 11 ECONOMIC EVIDENCE MODEL

## 1. Boundary

Workforce operates under economic reality but does not replace Economy & Treasury.

Workforce must preserve sufficient operational evidence for downstream economic processing.

## 2. Minimum economic evidence

For materially economic work, correlate where applicable:

```text
worker
organization context
work
role
assignment
time
capacity
resources
staffing
planned cost basis
actual cost basis/reference
outcome
```

## 3. Planned versus actual

The model must distinguish:

- planned labor hours vs actual labor hours;
- planned headcount vs actual available headcount;
- planned resource usage vs actual resource usage;
- planned cost vs actual cost/reference.

Workforce operational metrics may be calculated locally, but authoritative accounting remains Economy-owned.

## 4. Reality constraints

Workforce must preserve finite:

- workers;
- working time;
- availability;
- capacity;
- staffing;
- resources;
- attention;
- operating windows.

A simulation or plan must not silently fabricate unlimited staffing or capacity.

## 5. Capacity semantics

```text
Available Capacity
=
Scheduled Capacity − Unavailable Capacity
```

```text
Remaining Capacity
=
Available Capacity − Committed Capacity
```

```text
Coverage Ratio
=
Available Capacity / Required Capacity
```

Example:

```text
Required labor = 192 hours
Available labor = 64 hours
Coverage = 33.3%
Deficit = 128 hours
```

The deficit must remain visible rather than being silently converted into assumed completion.

## 6. Staffing evidence

The model must distinguish:

```text
Required Staffing
Scheduled Staffing
Available Staffing
Actual Staffing
Coverage
Shortage
Surplus
```

Example:

```text
Required  = 24
Scheduled = 20
Available = 18
Actual    = 16
```

This must not be represented simply as `Workers = 24`.

## 7. Resource evidence

Work may consume:

- labor;
- equipment;
- facilities;
- materials;
- energy;
- capital;
- time;
- organizational attention.

Consumption must be attributable to work/assignment/context where materially relevant.

## 8. P&L boundary

Workforce contributes operational evidence for:

- labor;
- capacity;
- staffing;
- resources;
- planned/actual operational usage;
- cost basis/reference.

Economy remains authoritative for accounting, valuation, treasury, settlement, and financial records.

## 9. Economic provenance

Economic evidence must preserve lineage to the Worker, organization/context, role, work, assignment, time, resources, and outcome that produced the operational consequence.

## 10. Implementation constraint

These are semantic evidence requirements, not an accounting schema. Workforce must not create a competing ledger.
