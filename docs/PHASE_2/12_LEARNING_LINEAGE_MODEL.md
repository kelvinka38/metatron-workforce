# METATRON WORKFORCE — PHASE 2 / 12 LEARNING LINEAGE MODEL

## 1. Purpose

Phase 2 does not implement full learning. It preserves the lineage required for Phase 8 learning and self-improvement.

## 2. Canonical closed loop

```text
WORK
 ↓
EXECUTION
 ↓
OBSERVATION
 ↓
OUTCOME
 ↓
EVIDENCE
 ↓
EXPERIENCE
 ↓
REFLECTION
 ↓
EVALUATION
 ↓
IMPROVEMENT CANDIDATE
 ↓
VALIDATION
 ↓
ADOPTION
 ↓
FUTURE BEHAVIOR
 ↓
NEW EXECUTION
```

## 3. Required lineage

```text
execution_id
   ↓
outcome_id / observation_id / evidence_id
   ↓
experience_id
   ↓
reflection_id
   ↓
evaluation_id
   ↓
improvement_candidate_id
   ↓
validation_id
   ↓
adoption_reference
   ↓
future execution comparison
```

## 4. Semantic distinctions

```text
OBSERVATION ≠ EXPERIENCE
EXPERIENCE ≠ REFLECTION
REFLECTION ≠ LEARNING
LEARNING ≠ KNOWLEDGE
KNOWLEDGE ≠ POLICY
```

Worker learning is not automatically institutional Knowledge. Knowledge admission remains governed externally.

## 5. Experience

Experience is derived from attributable execution, outcome, observation, and evidence. It must preserve lineage to those sources.

## 6. Reflection

Reflection evaluates experience and may identify:

- pattern;
- failure;
- variance;
- causal hypothesis;
- successful strategy;
- inefficient behavior;
- improvement opportunity.

Reflection is not automatically truth.

## 7. Learning

Learning is an evaluated Worker-associated adaptive state. It may affect strategy, heuristics, planning, communication, prioritization, or capability candidates within authorized boundaries.

Learning MUST NOT independently:

- create institutional authority;
- expand role authority;
- bypass policy;
- approve restricted operations;
- modify institutional governance;
- alter another domain's authority.

## 8. Improvement candidate

An improvement candidate is a proposed change derived from evidence. It is not an accepted improvement merely because behavior changed.

## 9. Baseline requirement

Improvement requires comparison against an appropriate:

- baseline;
- prior behavior;
- expected outcome;
- benchmark;
- counterfactual;
- validated metric.

Where no baseline exists, the result remains an improvement hypothesis.

## 10. Validation and adoption

```text
OBSERVED
 → REFLECTED
 → PROPOSED
 → EVALUATED
 → VALIDATED / REJECTED / INCONCLUSIVE
 → ADOPTED
```

Adoption does not automatically change institutional policy or authority.

## 11. Workforce-level learning

Evidence may be aggregated across Workers:

```text
Worker A ... Worker N
        ↓
Aggregated evidence
        ↓
Pattern
        ↓
Evaluation
        ↓
Workforce learning
        ↓
Candidate operational practice
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Adoption
```

Institutional adoption remains governed.

## 12. Implementation constraint

Phase 2 must preserve references and causal lineage sufficient for Phase 8. It does not implement model training, autonomous policy modification, or unrestricted self-modification.
