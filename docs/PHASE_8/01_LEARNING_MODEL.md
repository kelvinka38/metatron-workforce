# METATRON WORKFORCE — PHASE 8 / LEARNING MODEL

**Authority:** `METATRON WORKFORCE — MASTER EXECUTION PLAN.md`  
**Phase:** 8 — Learning / Self-Improvement  
**Status:** Implementation artifact

## 1. Objective

Represent institutional learning as a traceable consequence of real work, execution, observation, outcome, evidence, experience, reflection, evaluation, and validated improvement.

Learning is not generic conversational memory.

Learning MUST remain attributable to the operational reality from which it was derived.

## 2. Canonical lineage

``text
EXECUTION
    ↓
OBSERVATION
    ↓
OUTCOME
    ↓
EVIDENCE
    ↓
EXPERIENCE
    ↓
REFLECTION
    ↓
EVALUATION
    ↓
IMPROVEMENT CANDIDATE
    ↓
VALIDATION
    ↓
ADOPTION
    ↓
FUTURE BEHAVIOR
    ↓
NEW EXECUTION
``

## 3. Learning boundary

Workforce learning MAY preserve and derive institutional experience from attributable operational evidence.

Workforce learning MUST NOT:

- manufacture observations;
- manufacture outcomes;
- convert inference into fact;
- erase historical evidence;
- retroactively alter execution truth;
- silently change authorization;
- silently change policy;
- treat unvalidated improvement as adopted behavior.

## 4. Evidence requirement

Every material learning claim MUST identify its originating evidence and preserve provenance, attribution, and temporal validity.

An inference, estimate, hypothesis, or interpretation MUST remain distinguishable from observed operational fact.

## 5. Improvement lifecycle

An improvement candidate is NOT an adopted improvement.

Adoption requires explicit validation evidence and an attributable adoption decision.

The canonical distinction is:

``text
OBSERVED FACT
    ↓
EXPERIENCE
    ↓
REFLECTION
    ↓
IMPROVEMENT CANDIDATE
    ↓
VALIDATION
    ↓
ADOPTION
``

## 6. Historical integrity

Later learning MUST NOT rewrite the historical condition from which the learning was derived.

Corrections create new evidence or revised interpretation; they do not erase the original attributable record.

## 7. Future behavior

Adopted learning MAY influence future behavior only through an explicit, attributable mechanism.

Learning MUST NOT directly manufacture execution authority or execution feasibility.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Phase 8 Deliverable 1 is acceptable only when the implementation can demonstrate:

- [ ] Execution remains the origin of operational learning lineage.
- [ ] Observation remains distinguishable from interpretation.
- [ ] Outcome remains distinguishable from evidence about the outcome.
- [ ] Evidence remains attributable and time-valid.
- [ ] Experience references its originating evidence.
- [ ] Reflection remains distinguishable from experience.
- [ ] Evaluation remains distinguishable from reflection.
- [ ] Improvement candidates remain unadopted until validation.
- [ ] Validation evidence is attributable.
- [ ] Adoption is explicit.
- [ ] Future behavior references the adopted learning.
- [ ] Historical evidence remains reconstructable.
- [ ] Learning cannot manufacture authority.
- [ ] Learning cannot manufacture execution capability.
- [ ] Unvalidated learning cannot silently alter future execution.
